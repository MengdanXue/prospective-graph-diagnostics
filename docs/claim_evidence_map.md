# Route A Claim--Evidence Map

Updated: 2026-09-08. This map governs the evidence boundaries for the submitted manuscript, `main_neurocomputing.tex`, and the internal revision draft, `main_tmlr.tex`. Updating this document does not change the submitted manuscript or indicate a new submission. The two entry points reuse the same frozen benchmark; the revision is preparation for one paper, not an independently validated second study.

The central claim is: **The evaluated simple diagnostic lacks demonstrated incremental value under the tested protocol; its relative regret depends on the candidate portfolio in post-hoc observations.** Neither part establishes a universal failure of diagnostics, an architecture mechanism, or performance on unseen datasets.

A numerical result may appear in the abstract only when its evidence is available and its wording preserves the analysis status below. Post-hoc results must be named as such. Record validation, unit tests, source hashes, and successful manuscript compilation establish different forms of consistency; they do not establish scientific validity, deterministic training, or external generalization. The internal response preparation is in [revision_response_pack.md](revision_response_pack.md).

## Revision narrative and reporting priorities

1. Define the operational graph-portfolio versus MLP decision and the information available at decision time.
2. State the frozen protocol, its exploratory-origin threshold, the asymmetric portfolio budget, and the failure to demonstrate incremental value.
3. Report the post-hoc change in relative regret across candidate portfolios, immediately including all-subset and dataset-composition limits.
4. Use two-dataset raw sensitivity to disclose that the training recipe affects the estimand; do not turn validation-only development results into new held-out evidence.
5. Retain the three-dataset degree-preserving intervention as auxiliary graph-signal evidence and the fixed-degree calculation as a scoped surrogate result.
6. Reserve broader selector, equal-compute, mechanism, and external-generalization claims for separately designed evidence. More seeds or a successful record audit cannot supply missing independent datasets.

The response pack anticipates concerns and links their evidence; it is not a record of actual reviewer requests or completed journal correspondence. The current source and data identifiers document provenance, while inaccessible private history remains an explicit reproducibility boundary. The frozen benchmark is available in the existing public release; including the new diagnostic summaries in a local revision package does not establish their public release. Record reconstruction and training repeatability are separate claims.
