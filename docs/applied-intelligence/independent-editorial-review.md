This record covers the editorial adaptation from the preserved TMLR source at

record or permission to submit or train.

## Scope

The independent check read the original protocol, primary results, sensitivity
analysis, introduction, discussion and conclusion. It also read the Applied
Intelligence entry, introduction, discussion and conclusion after adaptation.
Edits by this checker were limited to `sections_applied/02_related_work.tex` and
`sections_applied/04a_equal_budget_sensitivity.tex` at that stage.

## Scientific boundaries checked

- The historical paper-level rule did not uniquely designate always-graph or
  specify that every baseline must be beaten. Always-graph is the report's
  focal comparator, not a uniquely preregistered one.
- Combined's 7.46-point loss belongs to the complete policy with MLP fallback.
  The 75.9% abstention contribution is not a pure diagnostic effect.
- With complete inputs, MLP fallback makes the full-set action a homophily
  threshold; the 0.40 validation cutoff affects coverage.
- Threshold selection followed legacy aggregate inspection and preceded the
  primary records; whole-project preregistration is not claimed.
- Holm resolution is bounded by the fixed comparison family and dataset
  composition. Non-rejection does not establish equivalence.
- Four trials per architecture leaves 24 graph trials versus four MLP trials.
  Single-architecture restrictions do not equalize compute.
- Favorable GCN/GAT subset averages disappear after joint Cornell/Wisconsin
  exclusion. Portfolio and threshold comparisons remain post-hoc.
- Two-dataset retraining is not a full-benchmark replay. The normalized-feature
  rerun gaps differ from the original recorded gaps.
- Fallback, exclusion and preprocessing analyses are kept distinct rather
  than pooled into a corrected effect size.
- The new 11-dataset and MLP-budget extensions were not treated as completed
  research results in this adaptation.

No new scientific overclaim or numerical conflict was found in the entry,
introduction, discussion and conclusion within this bounded check. The source
owner subsequently confirmed that the author explicitly supplied the contact
email and absence of research funding, resolving the declaration question.

This editorial check does not certify every experiment, raw-data reconstruction,
checkpoint replay, publication requirement, or the complete submission package.
