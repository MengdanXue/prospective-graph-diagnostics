For the descriptive regret--coverage curves, the frozen scorer maps a margin $v$ and scale $s$ to $c(v;s)=\min\{1,|v|/s\}$. Here, $a_{\mathrm{val}}^{\mathrm{G}}$ and $a_{\mathrm{val}}^{\mathrm{M}}$ are the validation accuracies of the selected graph and MLP portfolios. Validation selection uses $v=a_{\mathrm{val}}^{\mathrm{G}}-a_{\mathrm{val}}^{\mathrm{M}}-\delta$ and $s=0.25$. On the Combined graph branch, $v=h_1-0.55$ and $s=0.45$; on its MLP branch, $v=\max\{0.55-h_1,a_{\mathrm{val}}^{\mathrm{M}}-0.40\}$ and $s=0.60$. Abstentions receive no confidence score and are excluded from the descriptive curve. These scores determine only the curve ordering; they do not alter actions or the primary full-set inference.
