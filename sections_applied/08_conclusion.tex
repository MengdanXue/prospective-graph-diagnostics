\section{Conclusion}

Graph-diagnostic value depends on the candidate portfolio and the tasks available for calibration. In this eleven-dataset audit, a training-label-only CPM adaptation improves over constant actions in restricted GCN/GAT portfolios but loses to always-graph in the full portfolio. Most of that loss comes from Roman-empire; omitting it reverses three of four comparisons. Adjusted homophily's LODO gains vanish or reverse under source-group holdout, with constant-graph decisions on both WebKB datasets.

Complete policies and dataset-level benefit and harm locate these conditional gains and losses; executable reconstruction makes the comparisons reproducible. Evaluating new selectors on independent graph sources will require explicit candidate-search budgets, fallback rules, and calibration access.
